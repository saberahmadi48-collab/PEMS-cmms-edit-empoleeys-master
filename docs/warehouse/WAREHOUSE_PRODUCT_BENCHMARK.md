## Recommended target process

- **Receiving:** Link the receipt and its lines to the purchase order where applicable. Record physical arrival and discrepancies. Route inspected quantities to quarantine or inspection, then release accepted stock and confirm its location. Give exceptional receipts a dedicated permission and audit reason.
- **Work-order supply:** Record demand and sourcing route, then distinguish requisition coverage, reservation, picking, physical issue, and return. Preserve the remainder after a partial issue, cancellation, or short close. Direct-purchase demand stays linked to the same work-order line.
- **Inventory control:** Record source, destination, quantity, unit, actor, time, and applicable lot or serial for each movement. Schedule cycle counts by criticality and usage. Review variances before posting adjustments and reconcile stock with the ledger and open reservations.
- **Visibility:** Offer one queue for open warehouse intake work. Show identified demand, reserved quantity, physical issue, and remaining demand as distinct measures.

## PEMS implementation sequence

1. **Protect balances and quality gates.** Address WH-01, WH-02, WH-03, and WH-06 from the [code audit](WAREHOUSE_CODE_AUDIT.md). Acceptance: repeat or concurrent submissions create no duplicate document or excess coverage; quarantined stock is unavailable; unauthorized operations are rejected.
2. **Close the demand-to-issue loop.** Address WH-04 and WH-05. Acceptance: a partial issue, cancellation, or rejected purchase branch remains visible and reconcilable against its source order.
3. **Pilot location and traceability controls.** Add or validate count tasks, variance review, and lot/serial evidence in one warehouse. Acceptance: trace a selected part from receipt to issue and back, and reconcile physical count, ledger, stock, and reservations.
4. **Expand by operating need.** Consider directed putaway, handling units, mobile picking, and project installation evidence after validating process volume and requirements.
